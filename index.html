Who We Are

Designing with earth, light & intention

Caveman Decorations is an architecture and interior design studio based in New Cairo. We believe great spaces are not about excess — they are about honesty of material, balance of form, and a deep respect for how people live.

Our work draws from organic shapes, natural textures, and a restrained earthy palette. Every project is a collaboration: we listen carefully, then shape environments that feel both timeless and distinctly yours.
